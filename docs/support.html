Support us

Keep the calm kit free.

No ads, no in-app purchases, nothing to unlock.

Every feature in Kairos is free. It works entirely on your phone and never asks you to sign up. There are no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases, and we'd like to keep it that way.

Building Kairos, testing it on each new version of Android, keeping its support lines up to date and answering bug reports all take time. If Kairos has helped you through a hard moment, a donation helps pay for that time. It's entirely optional, and every feature stays free either way.

Other ways to help: report a bug or suggest a feature, or tell a friend who might find it useful.
