# Delete your data: Kairos

**Kairos – Calm Tools**, by ToolCrate, has no accounts and no servers. Everything stays on your phone,
plus an encrypted backup in your own Google Drive if you turned that on. We (the developer) never
receive any of it, so there is nothing for us to delete and no request you need to send.
You can delete all of it yourself, at any time:

## 1. Delete the data on your phone

Uninstall **Kairos** (long-press its icon → **Uninstall**), or go to Android **Settings → Apps → Kairos → Storage → Clear storage**. This deletes everything Kairos keeps on your phone, straight away.

## 2. Delete the Google Drive backup (only if you turned it on)

1. In Google Drive on the web, open **Settings** (the gear) → **Manage apps**.
2. Find **Kairos** and choose **Delete hidden app data**.

To stop future backups as well, turn off Google Drive backup in Kairos → **Settings** → **Backup**.

Your backup is deleted from Google Drive straight away. Google may keep deleted data in its own
systems for a short time, as described in Google's privacy policy.

## What gets deleted

- The reassurance cards you wrote
- Names and phone numbers of the trusted contacts you chose
- A history of sessions: when you used urge or panic help, how an urge went if you said, and the optional 1 to 5 check-in
- Your settings, and your app-lock PIN (stored only as a one-way hash; never in the backup)

Nothing is kept anywhere else: Kairos has no analytics, no crash reports and no other copies.
Exported files you saved yourself are yours to delete.
