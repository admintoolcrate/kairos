# Kairos

Calm tools for hard moments. Free, offline, no ads, no accounts.

- **An urge:** a 10 or 20 minute timer, slow breathing and a few things to do while you wait.
- **Panic:** very large text, a slow breathing circle that never asks you to hold your breath,
  5-4-3-2-1 grounding, cards in your own words, and one tap to call someone.
- Works fully offline. Optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive.

Kairos is a self-help tool. It isn't medical care, and it can't diagnose or treat anything.
If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, or think you may be in danger, call your local
emergency number.
