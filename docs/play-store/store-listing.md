# Store listing (draft)

Rules for all store text: no medical claims, no diagnosis, no "treats", "cures", "therapy",
"clinically", and no promises that a feeling will pass. Describe what the app does.

**App name** (max 30): `Kairos`

**Short description** (max 80):
`Calm tools for hard moments. Free, offline, no ads, no accounts.`

**Full description** (max 4000):

> Kairos is a quiet place for the hard moments: an urge or craving you don't want to act on,
> or panic. Open it from your home screen, the Quick Settings tile, or even the lock screen.
>
> **An urge**
> Set a 10 or 20 minute timer and give the urge some time. A calm line to read, a minute of
> slow breathing, and a few things to do while you wait. Afterwards, note how it went, if you
> want to.
>
> **Panic**
> Very large text and nothing to choose. Breathe with a slow circle, longer out than in, and
> never holding your breath. Walk through 5-4-3-2-1 grounding, one prompt at a time. Read
> cards you wrote earlier, in your own words. Call someone you trust, a support line, or
> emergency services with one tap.
>
> **Made to stay out of the way**
> • Free, with no ads, no accounts and no analytics
> • Works fully offline; nothing leaves your phone
> • Optional encrypted backup to your own Google Drive
> • Light and dark themes, large text, reduced motion and TalkBack support
> • Optional app lock for your history and settings. Panic help is never locked.
>
> Kairos is a self-help tool. It isn't medical care, and it can't diagnose or treat anything.
> If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, or think you may be in danger, call your local
> emergency number.
