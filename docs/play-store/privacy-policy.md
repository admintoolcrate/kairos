# Kairos privacy policy

*Last updated: 20 November 2026*

Kairos is a free app with calm tools for hard moments. It is made by
ToolCrate. This policy explains what happens to your information. The short version:
**everything stays on your phone, unless you choose to back it up to your own Google Drive,
and then it is encrypted with a passphrase only you know.**

## What Kairos keeps, and where

Kairos stores the following **only on your phone**, in the app's private storage:

- the reassurance cards you write;
- the names and phone numbers of people you choose as trusted contacts;
- your settings (for example breathing timing, theme, your support line);
- a simple history of sessions: when you used urge or panic help, how an urge went if you
  chose to say, and the optional 1 to 5 check-in.

There are no accounts. Kairos has no servers, and we (the developer) never receive any of it.

## What Kairos does not do

- No ads and no advertising ID.
- No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or third-party SDKs that collect data.
- No location, microphone, camera or contacts permissions. When you pick a trusted contact,
  Android lets Kairos read just that one name and number, once.
- No internet use at all, except the optional Google Drive backup below.

## Optional: backup to your own Google Drive

If you turn it on, Kairos saves one backup file to a hidden app folder in **your own Google
Drive** (the "app data folder"). Kairos asks Google only for access to that folder, and can't
see anything else in your Drive.

- The file is **encrypted on your phone before upload** (AES-256-GCM, with a key derived from
  your passphrase using PBKDF2-SHA256). Neither Google nor we can read it without your passphrase.
- Your app-lock PIN is never included.
- The upload goes directly from your phone to Google over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.
  It never passes through us.
- Google's handling of your Drive is covered by Google's own privacy policy.

To delete it: in Google Drive on the web, open **Settings → Manage apps**, find Kairos, and
choose **Delete hidden app data**. Uninstalling Kairos deletes everything on your phone.

## Exporting to a file

You can export your data to a file you choose. If you add a passphrase it is encrypted the
same way; without one it is plain text, so keep it somewhere private.

## Stagira

If you also use Stagira (by the same developer) and turn on "Record in Stagira", Kairos opens
Stagira on your phone to note an urge you rode out. Only the time is passed, and Stagira asks
you to confirm. Nothing goes over the internet.

## Calls

When you tap a contact, a support line or emergency services, Kairos opens your phone's dialer
with the number filled in. Kairos never places calls itself and keeps no call records.

## Children

Kairos is not directed at children under 13.

## Not medical care

Kairos is a self-help tool. It isn't medical care, and it can't diagnose or treat anything.
If you have chest pain, trouble breathing, or think you may be in danger, call your local
emergency number.
